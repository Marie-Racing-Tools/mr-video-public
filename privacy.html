Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

mr-video is a private tool operated by Marie Racing Tools for its owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and collects no data from visitors.

  • What is stored: after the owner logs in to a platform, the platform's access token for that account is stored on the owner's own server. It is used only to send the owner's videos to that account.
  • What is shared: the videos the owner chooses to send go to the platform the owner chose. Nothing is sold or passed to anyone else.
  • Analytics and cookies: this page uses none.
  • Deletion: the owner can remove the access token on the server and revoke the app's access in the settings of the platform account at any time.
